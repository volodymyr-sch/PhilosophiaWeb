Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

This website has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no comments. Google Analytics runs only if you click Allow analytics. We do not use analytics for advertising.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the website philosophiaquotes.com (the “Website”). Volodymyr Shcherbiuk runs the Website. In this policy, “we” means Volodymyr Shcherbiuk.

The Philosophia mobile app has its own policy. Read the privacy policy of the mobile app.

What data the Website handles

Hosting data

Netlify hosts the Website. When your browser asks for a page, the Netlify servers process technical data. This data includes your IP address, the page you asked for, the date and time, and your browser type. Every web server needs this data to send pages and to stay secure. We do not use it to identify you. Read the privacy policy of Netlify.

Analytics data (only with your permission)

When you open the Website for the first time, a notice asks if you allow analytics. Until you click Allow analytics, we load no Google Analytics code, and Google Analytics sets no cookies.

If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure:

  • the pages you visit;
  • clicks on the App Store and Google Play buttons;
  • the page that sent you to the Website;
  • general device, browser, and region data that Google Analytics collects.

Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_… to tell visits apart. Google acts as our service provider for this measurement. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

We keep the advertising features of Google off. We do not use Google Signals. We do not use ad personalization. We do not use analytics for advertising.

Your choice on your device

The Website saves your analytics choice in the local storage of your browser. The entry is named philosophia-analytics-consent. It holds the word “granted” or “denied”. It stays on your device. We do not receive it.

E-mail

If you write to us, we use your e-mail address only to answer you.

Change your choice

Use the Analytics settings button in the footer of any page. You can allow or decline analytics at any time. If you decline, the Website removes the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you or delete data that we hold about you. Write to philosophia.application@gmail.com. We answer within a reasonable time.

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we use analytics only with your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Google Analytics keeps data for a limited period that we set in our Analytics account.

Links to other sites

The Website links to the App Store, Google Play, and sites that host source texts. These sites have their own privacy policies. We do not control them.

Children

The Website is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Google data in the owner’s private tool

The Website owner uses a private desktop tool named SEOMaster. The tool reads the reports of this Website in Google Search Console and Google Analytics. Only the owner uses it. It is not offered to the public.

  • The owner signs in with a Google account and allows read-only access to these reports.
  • The tool stores the reports and the sign-in token on the owner’s own computer.
  • The tool does not share this data, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising.
  • The tool cannot change anything in Google Search Console or Google Analytics.
  • The owner can remove the access at any time in the permissions of the Google account.

The use of information that SEOMaster receives from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

We can change this policy. We publish the new text on this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to philosophia.application@gmail.com.
