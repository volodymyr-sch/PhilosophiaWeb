Interpretation

What this David Hume quote asks you to notice

The operative phrase is “Imagination has unbounded liberty in combining ideas.” Treat this line as an invitation to inspect a belief. Identify what it claims we can know, what evidence would challenge it, and whether the author is describing certainty, practical judgment, or intellectual humility.

This page does not treat one sentence as a complete account of David Hume’s philosophy. It uses the quote as a reading prompt: identify the claim, test its limits, and follow the source before applying it as advice.

About David Hume

David Hume (1711-1776) was a Scottish philosopher, historian, and essayist who became one of the most influential figures in Western philosophy through his radical empiricism and skepticism. Born in Edinburgh to a modest but noble family, Hume's ideas challenged traditional metaphysics and revolutionized our understanding of human knowledge and moral psychology.
