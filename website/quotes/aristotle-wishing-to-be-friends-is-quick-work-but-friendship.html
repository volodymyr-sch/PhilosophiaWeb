Interpretation

What this Aristotle quote asks you to notice

The operative phrase is “Wishing to be friends is quick work but friendship.” Ask what this line means by a good life: a feeling, a pattern of action, a relationship, or a judgment about value. Compare that answer with your own before turning the quote into a rule for happiness.

This page does not treat one sentence as a complete account of Aristotle’s philosophy. It uses the quote as a reading prompt: identify the claim, test its limits, and follow the source before applying it as advice.

About Aristotle

Aristotle (384-322 BCE) was a Greek philosopher and scientist who is considered one of the greatest intellectual figures in Western history. Born in Stagira, a Greek colony in Macedonia, he was the son of a physician to the Macedonian royal court. This background likely influenced his empirical approach to knowledge and his interest in biology.
