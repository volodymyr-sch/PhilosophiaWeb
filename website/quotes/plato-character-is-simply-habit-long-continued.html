Interpretation

What this Plato quote asks you to notice

The operative phrase is “Character is simply habit long continued.” Read this as an ethical proposal rather than a decorative maxim. Ask what kind of person or action it commends, which competing duties it leaves unstated, and how the principle would look in a small decision today.

This page does not treat one sentence as a complete account of Plato’s philosophy. It uses the quote as a reading prompt: identify the claim, test its limits, and follow the source before applying it as advice.

About Plato

Plato (c. 428-348 BCE) was one of the most influential philosophers in Western history, whose works laid the foundation for much of Western philosophy, science, mathematics, and political theory. Born into a noble Athenian family during the Golden Age of Athens, Plato was a student of Socrates and a teacher of Aristotle, forming a crucial link in the development of Western philosophical thought. Plato's philosophical legacy is preserved in his numerous dialogues, which represent the most complete surviving corpus…
