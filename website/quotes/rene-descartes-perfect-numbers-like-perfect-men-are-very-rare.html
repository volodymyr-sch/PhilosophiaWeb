Interpretation

What this René Descartes quote asks you to notice

The operative phrase is “Perfect numbers like perfect men are very rare.” Read this as a claim about response under pressure, not as a promise that hardship is harmless or fully controllable. Ask which part concerns action, which part concerns judgment, and what the line might overlook in another person’s circumstances.

This page does not treat one sentence as a complete account of René Descartes’s philosophy. It uses the quote as a reading prompt: identify the claim, test its limits, and follow the source before applying it as advice.

About René Descartes

René Descartes (1596-1650) was a French philosopher, mathematician, and scientist who revolutionized Western philosophy and laid the foundations for modern philosophical thought. Often called "the father of modern philosophy," Descartes was instrumental in developing the scientific method and bridging the gap between medieval scholasticism and modern philosophy. A three-step reflection

  1. Name the event without adding an interpretation.
  2. Separate the response you can choose from conditions you cannot command.
  3. Choose one proportionate action that preserves both courage and care.
