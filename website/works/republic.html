How to use the Source Lens

Source labels in the public catalog identify the work, and some identify a book or section. They do not identify an edition, translator, or page. Use the label as a locator, then verify the wording and context in the edition you actually cite.

  1. Open the complete work and locate the passage or nearest matching formulation.
  2. Read the surrounding chapter, letter, or exchange.
  3. Record the translator, edition, and stable section number before sharing it as a quotation.

Build a work-based collection

Save these passages together in Philosophia, then group your notes by recurring question rather than by how motivational a sentence sounds. A work-based collection helps a reader see development, disagreement, and qualification across multiple passages.
